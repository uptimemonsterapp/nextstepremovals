Apartment Moving Specialists in Perth

Moving into or out of an apartment comes with its own unique challenges — narrow hallways, shared lifts, staircases, parking restrictions and strata regulations. At Next Step Removals, we've mastered the art of apartment removals in Perth. Our team is trained to work efficiently in confined spaces while protecting common areas, lifts and walls from any damage.

Whether you're relocating from a high-rise in the Perth CBD, a townhouse in Leederville or a unit in Scarborough, our apartment moving service is tailored to the specific logistics of multi-dwelling buildings. We coordinate with building managers, reserve service lifts and ensure your move complies with all strata requirements.

Apartment Removal Services We Provide

  • High-rise apartment moves — Experienced with service lifts, loading docks and high-rise logistics.
  • Stair-access moves — Specialist equipment and techniques for walk-up apartments.
  • Townhouse & unit relocations — Perfect for smaller homes with limited access.
  • Building protection — Floor runners, wall protectors and lift padding to prevent damage.
  • Strata coordination — We handle booking service lifts and common-area access.

Perth Areas We Service

Our apartment removals team regularly works in Perth's key apartment precincts including the CBD, West Perth, Northbridge, Subiaco, Joondalup, Fremantle, Scarborough, Victoria Park and East Perth. We know the buildings, the rules and the best ways to get your move done quickly and cleanly.

The Next Step Difference

Unlike general removalists who struggle with apartment logistics, we specialise in them. Our compact, manoeuvrable trucks are ideal for city streets, and our team is trained in tight-space handling. Every Perth apartment move is fully insured, with transparent pricing and no hidden fees for stairs or lifts.
